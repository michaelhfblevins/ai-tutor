# Appendix J Physical constants and data

|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

| Mean radius            | $R_E = 6.37 \cdot 10^6 \text{ m}$                                                            |
| Mean density           | $5.52 \text{ g/cm}^3$                                                                        |
| Surface acceleration   | $g = 9.81 \text{ m/s}^2$                                                                     |
| Mean distance from sun | $1.5 \cdot 10^{11} \text{ m}$                                                                |
| Orbital speed          | $29.8 \text{ km/s}$                                                                          |
| Period of rotation     | $23 \text{ h } 56 \text{ min } 4 \text{ s} = 8.6164 \cdot 10^4 \text{ s}$                    |
| Period of orbit        | $365 \text{ days } 6 \text{ h} = 3.16 \cdot 10^7 \text{ s} \approx \pi \cdot 10^7 \text{ s}$ |
